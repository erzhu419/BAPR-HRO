Comments to the Author
I am keeping this review very brief because there are some red flags for irresponsible AI usage, and I consequently recommend rejection. 

- I was initially suspicious because the introduction had many quirks typical of low-quality AI writing. 
- Then, I skipped to the technical sections, and found Section 6 (up to Cor 1) to contain completely standard, known optimal transport results, stated as theorems with no citations to a typical reference.
- I am familiar with a fair bit of bandits literature but noticed a reference that I hadn't heard of "Chen Y, Krause A, Lattimore T (2024) Non-stationary bandits with adversarial corruptions in transit networks. Proc.  European Conference on Machine Learning (ECML)." As far as I can tell, this reference doesn't exist! I would not be surprised if there are other reference issues.
- The Wasserstein DRO results (Prop 1, Cor 1) are either trivial and lacking proper citations or false, depending on how generous one is with incomplete assumptions. (Assumption 3, only giving Lipschitzness of the function arr, is not sufficient for the stated results)
